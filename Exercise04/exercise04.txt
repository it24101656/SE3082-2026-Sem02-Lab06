1. Describe how the threads are generated for this code.

#pragma omp parallel for distributes the outer r loop among the 4 threads. 
Each thread gets some r values, and for each assigned r value it runs all 10 k iterations.

2. When you replace the code below with #pragma omp parallel for collapse(2) how are the threads generated.

When collapse(2) is used, OpenMP combines both the r and k loops into one larger iteration space. 
Since there are 5 × 10 = 50 combinations, these 50 iterations are distributed among the 4 threads.
